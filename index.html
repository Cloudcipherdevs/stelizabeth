Our Story of Care: Serving Nakuru with Compassion

St Elizabeth Medical Centre is a Level 4 private, faith-based hospital established to meet the urgent need for accessible, high-quality, and ethical healthcare in Nakuru County.

Mission

To provide high-quality, compassionate, and affordable healthcare services guided by Christian values, meeting the holistic needs of the Nakuru community.

Vision

To be the most trusted and preferred Level 4 faith-based hospital in the Rift Valley region, recognized for excellence in patient care and service.

Our Core Values

  • **Compassion, Integrity, Excellence, Accessibility, Faith.**

Comprehensive Healthcare Services for the Whole Family

We are equipped to handle your family's health needs from prevention and routine care to diagnostics and treatment.

General Medicine

Diagnosis and treatment of acute and chronic illnesses, routine check-ups, and managing non-communicable diseases.

Maternity and Antenatal Care

Safe delivery services, specialized antenatal clinics, postnatal care, and comprehensive family planning services.

Outpatient and Consultation

Walk-in consultations, follow-up appointments, wound dressing, and minor injury care available 24 hours.

Laboratory and Diagnostic Services

State-of-the-art laboratory testing, X-ray, and Ultrasound services for accurate and rapid diagnosis.

Minor Surgery

Safe and sterile environment for minor surgical procedures and day-case operations, handled by experienced surgeons.

Child Immunization & Family Health

Adherence to the KEPI immunization schedule, children's wellness clinics, and general family health maintenance.

Preventive Health Programs

Screening packages, health education, diet and lifestyle counseling to help you stay healthy and avoid illness.

24/7 Pharmacy Services

Access to a wide range of quality, certified medicines around the clock with guidance from our pharmacists.

Patients & Billing Information

We strive to make your hospital experience seamless and worry-free by working with major insurance partners and providing transparent billing.

NHIF & Insurance Partners

We are a fully NHIF accredited (Code: 0019885) Level 4 Private Hospital. You can comfortably use your NHIF card for various services, including inpatient care, maternity, and outpatient packages.

We also partner with a growing list of reputable private insurance providers. Please call us ahead of your visit to confirm your specific coverage.

  • NHIF Accepted
  • Madison Insurance (Placeholder)
  • Jubilee Health (Placeholder)
  • UAP Old Mutual (Placeholder)

Accepted Payment Methods

We accept a variety of payment methods for co-pays, excesses, and self-pay services:

  • Cash Payment
  • Debit/Credit Cards (Visa/MasterCard)
  • Mobile Money (M-Pesa/Airtel Money)
  • NHIF & Approved Insurance Scheme Cards

Patient Rights & Privacy

Your privacy and comfort are our priority. We adhere strictly to patient confidentiality laws. You have the right to transparent information regarding your treatment and costs. Full details available at the administration desk.
